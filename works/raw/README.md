# Raw data folder

This folder stores the intermediate raw data generated by the pipeline scripts of this project, including:

- [`step_1_preparation.py`](https://github.com/BGI-SynBio/SPIDER-WEB/tree/main/works/step_1_preparation.py)
- [`step_2_simulations.py`](https://github.com/BGI-SynBio/SPIDER-WEB/tree/main/works/step_2_simulations.py)
- [`step_3_experiments.py`](https://github.com/BGI-SynBio/SPIDER-WEB/tree/main/works/step_3_experiments.py)
- [`step_4_supplements.py`](https://github.com/BGI-SynBio/SPIDER-WEB/tree/main/works/step_4_supplements.py)

49 data files are organised according to their 
corresponding workflow stage, analytical purpose, or experimental objective. 
They are provided to support flexible reuse and partial reproduction of the analyses. 
In particular, users who wish to reproduce the experiments from a specific starting point, 
inspect intermediate results, or reuse selected outputs for other purposes 
do not need to restart the entire workflow from 
[`step_1_preparation.py`](https://github.com/BGI-SynBio/SPIDER-WEB/tree/main/works/step_1_preparation.py).

Depending on the computing environment and available resources, 
individual workflow steps may take from several minutes to several hours or days. 
Providing these intermediate raw data files therefore helps reduce unnecessary recomputation 
and improves the replicability of the project.

Notably, the provided MD5 checksums are intended solely to verify the integrity of downloaded files.
If regenerated intermediate output files differ from their downloaded counterparts in MD5 checksum or file size, 
users are advised to load both files and compare their contents directly.
During our replication tests, we observed that certain dictionary-based data structures 
may yield different MD5 checksums when serialised using Python's pickle module, even when their contents are identical.
Therefore, differences in MD5 checksums or file sizes do not necessarily indicate differences in the underlying data.
